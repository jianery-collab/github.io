About关于我

Pricing is not a price tag.
It is a business system.

定价不是一个数字，
而是一套经营体系。

I'm Dr. Jan Y. Yang — Pricing Architect with over 20 years of experience helping companies build pricing as an organisational capability, not an afterthought.

Most consultants arrive after the product is built, fix the price tag, and leave. By then the decisions that shape pricing power — product design, customer targeting, GTM — have already been made. My work starts earlier.

I embed pricing by design across the full business lifecycle: product ideation, customer segmentation, go-to-market, sales, pricing governance, and customer lifetime management. The result is a business with a real pricing system, not just a price.

Springer-published author. Based in Shanghai and Ingelheim am Rhein. Writing regularly on LinkedIn.

我是杨一安，定价架构师。二十余年来，我专注于帮助企业将定价能力真正内化为组织竞争力，而非事后补救的临时方案。

大多数顾问在产品上市后才介入，修改一下价格标签就离开。而此时，真正决定定价能力的关键早已尘埃落定：产品设计、客户定位、市场进入策略。我的工作，从更早开始。

我将定价以设计思维嵌入企业全生命周期：从产品构思与客户分层，到市场进入与销售执行，再到定价治理与客户长期价值管理。最终交付的不只是一个价格，而是一套可持续运转的定价体系。
