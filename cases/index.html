Automotive & Mobility

What I typically see

  • “Competitors are packing every feature into the car. We feel we have to follow, but we can’t afford to.”
  • “We are adding a new digital feature. How should we price and sell it: one-off, bundled, subscription? The options are many and confusing.”
  • “Our mobility-sharing pricing has grown over the years and now has nine elements. We think it may be too complicated, but we don’t know how or where to trim.”

What I deliver

  • Feature and package strategy
  • Digital feature monetisation
  • Willingness-to-pay research
  • Price model simplification
  • Offering strategy and launch roadmap

From past work

A premium car brand

Challenge
New to electric vehicles and to digital features, the brand lacked the know-how and the customer insight to design and price these offerings.
Approach
We first clarified what the company wanted its new digital offerings to achieve. We then assessed what customers and prospects expect: what they want, and what they are actually willing to pay. On that basis, we designed the offering strategy and an implementation roadmap.
Outcome
A clear, shared understanding of what the brand wants to achieve and what it needs to do. The first features launched as pilots in three markets.

Industrial & B2B

What I typically see

  • “So many people are involved in pricing, but no one is really responsible for it.”
  • “We have tens of thousands of spare parts, managed by hand. Our pricing is a mess.”
  • “Our pricing is too front-loaded. Each decision may make sense when the sales manager makes the quote. Taken together, we cannot reasonably explain a 25 to 50% price spread across similar deals.”

What I deliver

  • End-to-end pricing process design
  • Pricing organisation and governance
  • Profit leakage and price waterfall analysis
  • Spare parts pricing
  • Deal pricing and discount guidance

From past work

A global industrial group

Challenge
Pricing was all over the place. Each business unit had its own pricing function, and each region its own pricing habits. This autonomy was costing the company profit potential and raising questions about price fairness.
Approach
We took stock of pricing challenges and opportunities, then took immediate measures to stop the heaviest profit leakage. We streamlined the pricing process and organisation, and built a single source of truth as the backbone for business intelligence. Finally, we piloted an end-to-end price management framework in one business unit.
Outcome
A shared vision of price management. Immediate measures delivered an annualised profit impact of about 5% RoS. A successful pilot for the other business units to follow.
